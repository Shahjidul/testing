About Us

Welcome to Embedded Store, your one-stop destination for all things embedded systems! We are passionate about providing high-quality products and resources to help you bring your embedded projects to life. Whether you're a hobbyist, student, or professional, we have everything you need to succeed in the world of embedded systems.

Our Mission

Bangladesh is making great leaps and displaying promising potential in the technological field. In this era of innovation, the first movers and the fastest in adopting technology emerge as the winners. At the same time, a lot of trial and research is required to bring on this technological transformation. In our country, we are in no shortage of formidable talents in the field of science and technology. However, many of us lack adequate access to the right resources to further our research or materialize innovative ideas. In the search for the right resources, we find ourselves getting lost in the endless red tape and timely procedures, which are also costly, not to say the least. Despite the drawbacks, we aim to create an environment that supports innovation and fuels growth. This is why we want to make engineering equipment available to students and researchers without much hassle and at an affordable budget. Gone are the days of students having to pay excessive prices for each of their project components. In the long run, we want Bangladesh to be at the forefront and be a formidable force in the field of science, technology and innovation. Join us in this dream. It starts with YOU.
